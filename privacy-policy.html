Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to EduPath. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it and what choices you have. By using EduPath, you agree to the practices described on this page.

Information We Collect

EduPath does not require you to create an account to read our articles. We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you give us: if you contact us through our contact form (processed by FormSubmit) or email, we receive your name, email address and the message you send.
  • Newsletter details: if you subscribe to our newsletter, we receive the email address you enter.
  • Usage information: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data such as your browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

We use this information only to reply to your messages, improve our website and keep it secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. EduPath itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not use analytics cookies. However, some third-party services we use (listed below) may set technical cookies or read basic browser information when their content loads. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the website will continue to work normally.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run this website:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting): GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type for security and performance. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (icon library): our icons (Font Awesome) are loaded from cdnjs, which receives your IP address when the file is requested. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit (contact and newsletter forms): when you submit a form, the details you enter are sent through FormSubmit to our email inbox. See the FormSubmit Privacy Policy.
  • External links: our articles and resources page link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

EduPath does not currently display third-party advertising. If this changes, we will update this policy before ads are shown.

How Long We Keep Your Information

Messages sent through the contact form are kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up. Newsletter email addresses are kept until you ask us to unsubscribe.

Your Rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any personal information you have sent us, or to remove your email from our newsletter list, by emailing chandazahra0@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

EduPath is written for students of all ages but does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email chandazahra0@gmail.com or use our contact page.
