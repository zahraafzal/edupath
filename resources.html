Free Study Resources

These are free, trusted tools and websites we recommend for students. Each one is run by a well-known organisation. Links open the official website.

Online Courses

Khan Academy

Free lessons and practice in maths, science, economics and more, from school to early university level.

khanacademy.org

MIT OpenCourseWare

Lecture notes, videos and exams from real MIT courses, free for anyone.

ocw.mit.edu

Coursera

University courses online. Many can be audited for free; certificates usually cost extra.

coursera.org

Study Tools

Anki

A flashcard app that uses spaced repetition to help you remember facts for the long term.

apps.ankiweb.net

Desmos

A free online graphing and scientific calculator, great for maths practice.

desmos.com

Research and Writing

Google Scholar

Search engine for academic papers, books and theses.

scholar.google.com

Purdue OWL

Free guides on essay writing, grammar and citation styles like APA and MLA.

owl.purdue.edu

Scholarships and Admissions

HEC Pakistan

Official list of national and foreign scholarships for Pakistani students.

hec.gov.pk

DAAD Scholarship Database

Search scholarships for studying and researching in Germany.

daad.de
